About OHA

Nearly four decades of credit investing — across cycles, markets and capital structures.

We were founded with the conviction that disciplined, fundamental research, applied across the breadth of the credit markets, would deliver durable outcomes for our investors.

Our history

Built one cycle at a time.

From a small group of credit specialists in 1987 to a 400-person global platform, our story has been one of patient compounding.

  1. 1987

    Founding

    Oak Hill Advisors begins investing in non-investment-grade credit, applying fundamental research to high yield bonds and bank loans.

  2. 2001

    Entering the CLO market

    OHA sponsors its first CLO, becoming one of the earliest participants in what would grow into a critical pillar of the global credit ecosystem.

  3. 2008–2010

    Cycle-tested

    The global financial crisis validates OHA's emphasis on capital preservation, fundamental underwriting and patient deployment in distressed markets.

  4. 2014

    European expansion

    Build-out of the firm's European credit and CLO platforms, anchored by the London office.

  5. 2021

    T. Rowe Price acquires OHA

    The firm becomes a wholly-owned subsidiary of T. Rowe Price, combining specialist credit expertise with global distribution.

  6. 2026

    OFLEX launches

    OHA and T. Rowe Price introduce a multi-strategy credit interval fund, bringing institutional-grade credit access to U.S. wealth clients.
