The firm

A single platform for the full credit spectrum.

Oak Hill Advisors is a global credit-focused alternative asset manager with deep expertise across performing and distressed credit, high yield bonds, leveraged loans, private credit, structured products and direct lending.

We bring the rigor of fundamental, value-oriented research to every position we hold. Our portfolios are built one credit at a time — and our long-term partnerships with companies, sponsors and operators give us access to a proprietary opportunity set that few peers can match.

Since 2021, OHA has been a wholly-owned subsidiary of T. Rowe Price, combining the agility and credit-investment discipline of an independent specialist with the strength, stability and global distribution of one of the world's largest asset managers.

The OHA approach

Five principles that have guided our investing since day one.

Markets change. Cycles turn. The fundamentals of how we underwrite, structure and manage credit do not.

01

Fundamental, bottom-up research

Every position is the product of granular underwriting — capital structure, business durability, downside protection.

02

One firm, one investment process

Investment professionals across credit asset classes share a single research platform, single risk framework and single set of incentives.

03

Long-duration partnerships

Our relationships with sponsors, management teams and lenders span decades — generating proprietary opportunities others rarely see.

04

Risk discipline above all

We size positions for what can go wrong, not what can go right. Capital preservation is the foundation of long-term compounding.

05

Alignment with our investors

We are deeply co-invested with our clients — and we communicate with the candor that meaningful skin in the game demands.
