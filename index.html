The firm

A specialist credit manager, by design.

For nearly four decades, Oak Hill Advisors has applied fundamental, value-oriented research to the global credit markets — investing across private credit, high yield bonds, leveraged loans, distressed debt and structured credit.

Our portfolios are built one credit at a time. Long-term partnerships with companies, sponsors and operators give us access to a proprietary opportunity set, while a single integrated investment process binds the firm together across asset classes and geographies.

Since 2021, OHA has been a wholly-owned subsidiary of T. Rowe Price — combining the discipline of an independent specialist with the strength of a global asset manager.

Our approach

Five principles that have guided our investing since day one.

Markets change. Cycles turn. The fundamentals of how we underwrite, structure and manage credit do not.

Read the full philosophy
01

Fundamental research

Every position is the product of granular, bottom-up underwriting — capital structure, business durability, downside protection.

02

One investment process

Investment professionals across credit asset classes share a single research platform, single risk framework and single set of incentives.

03

Long-duration partnerships

Our relationships with sponsors, management teams and lenders span decades — generating proprietary opportunities others rarely see.

04

Risk discipline

We size positions for what can go wrong, not what can go right. Capital preservation is the foundation of long-term compounding.
