02 / Liquid Credit

High yield bonds & leveraged loans

1987
Investing since inception
38 yrs
Cycle-tested approach

OHA has been an active, value-oriented investor in the high yield and leveraged loan markets since the firm's founding. Our research process is designed to identify credits where we believe there is a meaningful probability of a positive credit-quality event.

We construct portfolios bond by bond and loan by loan, balancing relative-value opportunities against rigorous downside underwriting.

  • Global high yield bonds
  • Senior secured loans
  • Special-situations credit
  • Long-only & total-return mandates
  • Custom separately managed accounts
  • Multi-strategy credit
03 / Distressed

Stressed & distressed credit

5+
Major cycles invested through
2
Continents of coverage

Across both North American and European markets, OHA has invested in a broad range of distressed and stressed-credit situations — from traditional distressed leveraged loans and high yield bonds to rescue financings, debtor-in-possession lending, turnarounds and post-reorganization equities.

Our distressed investing benefits from being part of a larger credit platform: the same teams that lend to a company can step in when its capital structure becomes mispriced.

  • Distressed loans & bonds
  • Rescue financings & DIP lending
  • Process-driven distressed
  • Restructurings & reorganizations
  • Post-reorg equities
  • Opportunistic private equity
04 / Structured

Collateralized loan obligations

$31B+
Issuance since 2001
25 yrs
As a continuous CLO sponsor

OHA was one of the earliest participants in the CLO market and has continuously issued and managed transactions in both the United States and Europe for more than two decades.

We invest in CLOs we sponsor and manage, and selectively in third-party deals where we identify mispricing in mezzanine and equity tranches.

  • U.S. broadly syndicated CLOs
  • European CLOs
  • CLO equity & mezzanine investing
  • CLO warehouses
  • Static and managed structures
  • Cross-jurisdictional expertise
