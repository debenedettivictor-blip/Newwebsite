Strategies

A single platform across the global credit spectrum.

Liquid and illiquid. Performing and distressed. Senior and subordinated. We invest where the opportunity is — guided by one investment process and one risk culture.

OHA's private credit platform provides flexible, customized financing solutions to upper-middle-market and large-cap companies — most often in partnership with leading sponsors, founders and operators we have known for years.

02 / Liquid Credit

High yield bonds & leveraged loans

1987
Investing since firm inception
38 yrs
Cycle-tested approach

OHA has been an active, value-oriented investor in the high yield and leveraged loan markets since the firm's founding. Our research process is designed to identify credits where we believe there is a meaningful probability of a positive credit-quality event — a rating change, refinancing, IPO, M&A transaction or capital-structure restructuring.

We construct portfolios bond by bond and loan by loan, balancing relative-value opportunities against rigorous downside underwriting. The result: portfolios that aim to outperform broad benchmarks while protecting capital through cycles.

  • Global high yield bonds
  • Senior secured loans
  • Special-situations credit
  • Long-only and total-return mandates
  • Custom separately managed accounts
  • Multi-strategy credit
03 / Distressed

Stressed & distressed credit

5+
Major credit cycles invested through
2
Continents of distressed coverage

Across both North American and European markets, OHA has invested in a broad range of distressed and stressed-credit situations — from traditional distressed leveraged loans and high yield bonds to rescue financings, debtor-in-possession lending, turnaround opportunities and post-reorganization equities.

Our distressed investing benefits from being part of a larger credit platform: the same teams that lend to a company can step in when its capital structure becomes mispriced. That continuity of coverage is one of OHA's quiet competitive advantages.

  • Distressed leveraged loans & bonds
  • Rescue financings & DIP lending
  • Process-driven distressed
  • Restructurings & reorganizations
  • Post-reorg equities
  • Opportunistic private equity
04 / Structured

Collateralized loan obligations

$31B+
CLO issuance since 2001
25 yrs
As a continuous CLO sponsor

OHA was one of the earliest participants in the CLO market and has continuously issued and managed transactions in both the United States and Europe for more than two decades. Our scale as a credit manager — with hundreds of professionals covering the same loan universe — informs every CLO portfolio we build.

We invest in CLOs we sponsor and manage, and selectively in third-party deals where we identify mispricing in mezzanine and equity tranches.

  • U.S. broadly syndicated CLOs
  • European CLOs
  • CLO equity & mezzanine investing
  • CLO warehouses
  • Static and managed structures
  • Cross-jurisdictional expertise

Beyond corporate credit, OHA invests in a range of structured and specialty credit opportunities — including portfolio acquisitions, esoteric ABS, NAV-style financing for sponsors, and tailored capital solutions for financial institutions navigating regulatory or strategic change.

These investments draw on the same fundamental research engine that powers the rest of the platform, applied to less-traveled corners of the credit market.

  • NAV & sponsor-finance
  • Insurance-linked credit
  • Specialty & consumer finance
  • Custom capital solutions
